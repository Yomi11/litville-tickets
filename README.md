# Litville Tickets

Create a simple aesthetically pleasing ticketing platform for a music events production, livestream/broadcast and social media company, the name is Litville, the brand color is deep orange or purple and black. A sample to look at is Fever https://business.feverup.com/ However we are still in phase 1 which is physical venue tickets and the major thing we want to do on the website right now is just for users to complete a ticket purchase on our platform for an event we are hosting ourselves. Phase 1 should also include a payment API like paystack to complete payments. Phase 2 of the platform is event livestreaming and broadcast with users able to purchase both venue and livestream tickets (however focus is on phase 1). Problems to be addressed in phase 1 based on assumptions: virtual or livestream alternative to attending live music shows/concerts at venues; community-like platform for music fans/lovers i.e. give ratings, reviews on shows, concerts, singles, album drops, check music charts of the most popular charts: apple music, spotify, billboard; better ticketing experience for attendees i.e. cost-sharing, seat identification pre-event; ticketing solution for show organizers and promoters; flexibility for performing artists (cost for reduction to hosting concerts i.e livestream shows to a virtual audience or venue show with live audience).
